# CobbleCuisine

A cooking mod for Cobblemon on Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.1.

Cook meals in Cobblemon's Cooking Pot to change which Pokémon spawn around you, blend shakes and drinks in the
Mixer, and feed treats to your Pokémon.

## Meals

Every dish has a power. Eating it makes you Satiated, and the power lasts as long as the effect. Up to three
seasonings in the Cooking Pot add more powers or strengthen the dish's own, and several meals can be active at once.
Hold Shift over a food to see its powers, or over an ingredient to see what it adds as a seasoning.

| Food | Power |
|---|---|
| Curries | Attract Pokémon of one type |
| Sandwiches | Give spawned Pokémon a chosen Tera Type |
| Cakes | Attract Pokémon of one Egg Group |
| Salads | Make herds and Alphas more or less common |
| Candied berries | Better catch rate, shiny chance, EXP or IVs |
| Eclair and Fruity Flan | Giant or tiny Pokémon |
| Dubious Food | Keeps wild Pokémon away |

Seasonings include sauces (herds, Alphas, size, Hidden Abilities, longer meals), mints (natures), beans (Egg
Groups), roasted berries (types and Tera Types) and vitamins (EV yield).

## Feeding Pokémon

Beans raise friendship and can be grown from seeds found in grass. Poké Puffs and Malasadas raise friendship and
heal, and their seasonings make them stronger. Malasadas work best in a Pokémon's favourite flavour. Feeding fills
a Pokémon up, and a full Pokémon refuses more food.

## The Mixer

The Mixer is a craftable block in seven colours that runs on redstone power. It makes:

- Shakes for EV training. Three berries of one colour set the stat and strength, and seasonings add more EVs.
- Deluxe Shakes for levels, PP, Dynamax levels, egg moves and more.
- EV Yield drinks and Aprijuice.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- Fabric API
- Cobblemon 1.8

Optional: Mod Menu for the config screen, JEI to browse Mixer recipes, Catch Rate Display and Cobblemon Unchained.

Translations: English, Spanish (Ecuador), Japanese and Traditional Chinese.
