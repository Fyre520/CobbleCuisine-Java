# CobbleCuisine Changelog

## 2.2.0

### Meal powers
- Cooked meals now remember their powers: the dish's own power plus the powers of up to three seasonings.
- Eating a meal gives you the Satiated effect. Its powers stay active while it lasts, shown as effect icons.
- Several meals can be active at once (configurable, up to four). A newer meal replaces the same powers from older
  ones, and opposite meals (Tiny and Giant, Herd up and down, Alpha up and down) cancel each other.
- New powers: Herd Frequency up and down, Alpha Frequency up and down, Shiny Luck and Meal Duration.
- Type, Egg Group and EV Yield powers now recognise regional forms.
- Tiny and Giant meals follow Cobblemon's size range, and no longer resize Alphas.
- Catch Rate Display shows the correct catch boost from your meal.
- Grass no longer drops Galarica Nuts, Torchflowers or Torchflower Seeds.

### Foods and sauces
- New sauces: Sweet Honey, Sweet Fruit, Bitter Mushroom, Bitter Chocolate, Sour Milk and Spicy Honey, plus the
  Deluxe Sour Bean and Spicy Bean Sauces for seasoning.
- Every curry, sandwich, cake, salad and candied berry recipe has been reworked, and many accept a choice of
  ingredients.
- Eight new roasted berries.
- Retired dishes, as in earlier updates, can no longer be cooked and are marked [LEGACY]. Spice Mix and Salad Mix are retired.
- Renamed: Salty Bone Curry, Dry-Smoked Tail Curry, Salty Claw, Bitter Veggie and Bitter Fillet Sandwiches, and
  Jambon Beurre.

### Feeding Pokémon
- Poké Puffs and Malasadas heal and raise friendship by amounts that grow with their seasonings.
- Feeding food now fills a Pokémon up, and a full Pokémon refuses more.
- Fixed feeding setting a Pokémon's HP to the heal amount instead of adding to it.
- Reworked Poké Puff and Malasada recipes.

### Mixer
- New: the Mixer, a craftable block in seven colours for blending drinks and shakes. It runs on redstone power
  (configurable).
- Shakes are now made in the Mixer from three berries of one colour. The berries decide the stat and strength, and
  seasonings add extra EVs.
- Pokémon react to a shake's flavour: liked flavours give more EVs, disliked give fewer.
- The EV Yield drinks, Aprijuice and Deluxe shakes are now made in the Mixer.
- New setting: Shake EV Power (Low, Medium or High).

### Tooltips
- Hold Shift over a food to see its powers and their values, and over an ingredient to see what it gives as a
  seasoning.
- On servers, tooltips show the server's settings.

### Config
- New tabbed config screen with sliders, toggles and a help tip on every setting.
- Includes settings for Base/Seasoning values, Herds/Alphas, Multipliers, modifying Fullness increase for specific
  foods, Poké Puffs and Malasadas, meal Durations and Drop Rates.
